I'm Abdoulaye, a young developer building from Africa.

I'm creating Midday because I want founders and small businesses to have simple tools that make their work easier, clearer, and more organized.

My goal is to build useful financial products for Africa and, over time, for the rest of the world.

I'm learning, designing, coding, and improving in public — one step at a time.

This is only the beginning. Thank you for following the journey.
